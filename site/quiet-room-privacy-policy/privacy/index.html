Quiet Room Privacy Policy

Last updated: April 21, 2026 Applies to: Quiet Room Support: matthew.reinig@gmail.com

This Privacy Policy explains how Quiet Room collects, uses, stores, and shares information when you use the app, related AI features, and support services.

1. Who Operates Quiet Room

Quiet Room is operated by Matthew Reinig. For privacy, support, account, or app questions, email matthew.reinig@gmail.com or visit the support page.

2. Information We Collect

Depending on how you use the app, Quiet Room may collect:

  • Account and authentication data. Quiet Room creates an anonymous Firebase Auth session by default. If you sign in or create an account, we may receive account identifiers, email address, display name, provider information, and session data from email/password, Google, or Apple sign-in.
  • Messages and conversation data. We process the prompts you send, assistant replies, conversation titles, selected model, and related conversation metadata.
  • Profile and memory data. Quiet Room may derive and store profile or memory information from conversation content so future replies can keep continuity and personalization.
  • AI-sharing consent state. Quiet Room asks for consent before the first message is sent to the AI service. For signed-in users, this consent state is stored with the account. For anonymous users, it is stored locally on the device.
  • Technical metadata. We may process request IDs, user/account IDs, conversation IDs, model names, token usage, status, error categories, app/platform information, timing, and related operational metadata.
  • Voice-related request data. If you use voice playback, the text being turned into audio is sent to the voice service. Generated audio may be temporarily cached on your device for playback.
  • Support content. If you contact support, we collect the information you include in your message.

3. How We Use Information

  • Provide chat, account, and conversation-history functionality.
  • Maintain signed-in account state and app sessions.
  • Support continuity and personalization across conversations.
  • Generate AI chat responses and voice responses after consent.
  • Respond to support, privacy, account, and deletion requests.
  • Debug issues, protect the service, and maintain reliability.

4. AI Provider Use And Consent

Quiet Room uses OpenAI to generate chat responses, create voice responses, and help maintain derived profile or memory context. Conversation content, relevant prior context, selected profile/memory information, timezone context, model selection, and the text used for voice generation may be sent to OpenAI for those purposes.

Before your first message is sent to the AI service, Quiet Room shows a consent prompt. If you choose Not now, the pending message is not sent.

5. Third-Party Services

Quiet Room uses service providers that help operate the app, including:

  • Firebase Authentication for sign-in and account sessions.
  • Firebase Firestore for account-linked app data such as conversations, consent state, profile, and memory records.
  • OpenAI for AI-generated chat, profile/memory processing, and text-to-speech.
  • Hosting and logging infrastructure, such as backend hosting and CloudWatch or equivalent operational logging.
  • Vercel or equivalent static hosting for this public policy and support site.

We may also disclose information if required by law, to protect rights or safety, or as part of a business transition involving the product.

6. Retention

  • Account-linked product data. Conversation, profile, memory, and signed-in AI-consent data are retained until you delete the relevant conversation or delete your account, unless otherwise stated.
  • Operational logs. Deployed operational logs are metadata-first and are retained for up to 90 days, then expire automatically. Local development logs may persist until manually rotated or deleted.
  • Device-local data. Local sessions, consent cache, and temporary audio cache files may remain on your device until app cleanup, operating-system cache eviction, uninstall, or manual app-data clearing.

7. Account Deletion

Signed-in users can delete their account in the app. Open Quiet Room, tap the profile icon, choose Delete Account, and confirm. The backend deletes the Firebase Auth user and known Firestore user data, including conversations, memory/profile records, profile history entries, and signed-in AI-consent state under that user account.

Operational logs are not generally deleted individually during account deletion and may remain until the 90-day retention window expires. Device cache or app-local data may also remain until local cleanup, OS cache eviction, uninstall, or manual app-data clearing.

More details are available on the account deletion page.

8. Support And Debugging Access

Quiet Room does not rely on operational logs as the primary place to review conversations. Operational logs are meant to help locate request IDs, timing, status, model, error categories, and related metadata.

If support or debugging requires review of user content, review should be scoped to the relevant account or conversation data rather than broad log review.

9. Your Choices

  • You may choose whether to sign in or continue with an anonymous session.
  • You may choose whether to consent before sending messages to the AI service.
  • You may delete individual conversations where available.
  • You may delete a signed-in account from the profile icon menu.
  • You can remove app-local data by uninstalling the app or clearing app data on your device.
  • You can contact support at matthew.reinig@gmail.com.

10. Children

Quiet Room is not intended for children under 13, and we do not knowingly collect personal information from children under 13.

11. Changes To This Policy

We may update this Privacy Policy from time to time. When we do, we will revise the "Last updated" date on this page.

12. Contact

For privacy or support questions, contact matthew.reinig@gmail.com.
