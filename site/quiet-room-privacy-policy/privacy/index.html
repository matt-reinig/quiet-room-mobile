Quiet Room Privacy Policy

Last updated: April 11, 2026 Applies to: Quiet Room and Quiet Room QA Support: matthew.reinig@gmail.com

This Privacy Policy explains how Quiet Room may collect, use, store, and share information when you use the app and related support services.

1. Scope

This Privacy Policy applies to the Quiet Room mobile applications, including the production app distributed as Quiet Room and the QA app distributed as Quiet Room QA, along with this support website.

2. Information We May Collect

Depending on how you use the app, we may collect the following categories of information:

  • Account information. If you create an account or sign in, we may receive your email address and basic account identifiers from the authentication provider you use, including email/password or Google sign-in.
  • Content you provide. We may process prompts, messages, conversation titles, support requests, and other content you choose to submit through the app.
  • Conversation history. If you use a signed-in account, conversation history may be stored so your chats can be revisited across sessions and devices.
  • Technical and device information. We may receive basic technical information needed to run and secure the service, such as app version, platform, request metadata, and operational logs.
  • Local device data. The app may store limited local preferences on your device, such as playback or interface settings. The app may also temporarily cache audio files on-device for playback.

3. How We Use Information

  • Provide authentication and account access.
  • Deliver Quiet Room responses and conversation functionality.
  • Support audio playback and related app features.
  • Respond to support requests, feedback, and operational issues.
  • Maintain security, reliability, and policy compliance.
  • Improve the product and resolve bugs over time.

4. Anonymous and Signed-In Use

Quiet Room may allow limited use without a traditional registered account. Some features, such as preserving and revisiting conversations across sessions, may depend on signing in. If you sign in, information can be associated with your account so the service works consistently across use.

5. Audio and Microphone-Related Features

The app includes audio-related functionality, including playback of voice responses. Audio content used for playback may be retrieved from the service and temporarily cached on your device.

Quiet Room is not described as an always-listening app. If audio or microphone-related features change materially in the future, this policy will be updated accordingly.

6. How Information May Be Shared

We may share information with service providers that help us operate Quiet Room, such as providers used for:

  • authentication and account management,
  • cloud hosting and backend infrastructure,
  • storage and delivery of conversation or audio-related content, and
  • AI or model-backed response generation.

We may also disclose information if required by law, to protect rights or safety, or as part of a business transition involving the product.

7. Retention

We keep information for as long as reasonably necessary to operate the service, maintain security, comply with legal obligations, and support user requests. The exact retention period can vary depending on whether the data is tied to an account, an active conversation history, or a short-lived operational log.

8. Your Choices

  • You may choose whether to use available sign-in methods.
  • You can contact support to ask questions about your data.
  • If you want to request account or account-linked data deletion, contact us at matthew.reinig@gmail.com.
  • You can remove app-local data by uninstalling the app or clearing app data on your device.

9. Children

Quiet Room is not intended for children under 13, and we do not knowingly collect personal information from children under 13.

10. Changes To This Policy

We may update this Privacy Policy from time to time. When we do, we will revise the "Last updated" date on this page.

11. Contact

For privacy or support questions, contact matthew.reinig@gmail.com.
